The same maths in C++

Every number above comes from these five lines. The last one is the difference between the two verdicts.

PunchElapsed += DeltaTime;
float Alpha    = PunchElapsed / PunchDuration;
float Offset   = CurvePunch->GetFloatValue(Alpha) * PunchDistance;
FVector Desired  = PunchStartLocation + TargetBatDirection * Offset;
FVector Velocity = (Desired - Component->GetComponentLocation()) / DeltaTime;

Feeding Desired to SetRelativeLocation places the bat at the dots and nowhere between them. Feeding Velocity to SetPhysicsLinearVelocity makes the solver travel the line joining them, which is what the second verdict is testing.
