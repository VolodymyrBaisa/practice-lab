What you can type

leader
the filled dot, in unreal units
follower
the hollow dot, the segment behind it
spacing
the slider value, a plain number
delta
unlocked from step 2 onward
dir
unlocked from step 3 onward, holding exactly what you wrote in step 2
normalize(v)
same direction, length 1
length(v)
how long a vector is
yaw(v)
the angle of a vector, in degrees
(x, y)
writes a vector; also v.x and v.y

A vector times a number scales it. A vector times a vector is refused here, exactly as it is in C++.
